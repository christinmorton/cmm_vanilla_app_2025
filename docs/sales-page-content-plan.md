## 🔍 Step 2: Identify Hidden or Implicit Skills

Some things you do aren't obvious from the way they're listed. You're already performing or are capable of the following — they're valuable and should be made explicit:

- **Security Engineering**: VPC, Cloudflare, reCAPTCHA, and DDoS management imply solid security practices.
- **DevOps Lite**: You've manually deployed apps, configured Nginx, managed CI/CD via GitHub Actions.
- **Headless CMS Expertise**: Strapi, Contentful, and WordPress APIs show mastery of the headless CMS concept.
- **Cost-Saving Architecture**: You engineer around SaaS limitations, which is incredibly valuable to budget-conscious clients.
- **API Economy Ready**: You build and consume APIs across multiple frameworks — that means you're integration-ready.
- **Client-First Thinking**: Your mention of saving people money and customizing platforms shows a consultant mindset, not just a coder.
